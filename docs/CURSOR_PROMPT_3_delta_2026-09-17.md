STEP A — Department AREAS and two-layer department time (replaces the strip-based department minutes)

STEP D — Post-processing reconciliation (nightly, feeds per-person KPIs; never the aggregate ones)
----
- Port `lib/assoc.py` (`candidates`: window −3…12 s, ≤ 8 m walkable on the `70_topology` graph, ≤ 2 m/s required speed; `assign`: sequential windowed Hungarian, 30 s windows, one-to-one, no-match option, `min_score`, `min_margin`), `80_candidates.py`, `90_assign.py` (journeys, zones, classes, calibration via `--calib`). Journeys are stored in `daily_journeys(venue_id, day, journey_id, tracklet_keys, raw_person_ids, start_time, end_time, n_tracklets, confidence, class)` and `daily_journey_zones`.
- Pair scorer: the learned model of `190_learned_linker.py` ("mixed" variant): features = the relational set only (`FEAT_SAFE` in the script: gap, euclidean and walkable distance, ratio, required speed, stature differences dh/dbh, heading consistency, break-map priors occ_a/occ_b, zone code, topology flags, hour, candidate counts/ranks/relative distances per death and per birth, flow prior, hand score) — NEVER vendor velocities or absolute tracklet lengths (documented leak, §4). Training data each night: artificial fragmentation of long tracklets of the same day (continuous gaps 0.7–30 s, pieces ≥ 3 s, put back into the real pool) + vendor-confirmed pairs (ids sharing a `track_key`), 70/30 split by death; model = HistGradientBoosting (Python, sklearn 1.8) saved as `/data/hyperspace/models/<venue>/linker_<day>.pkl`; operating point = `min_score`/`min_margin` chosen on the artificial train set with precision ≥ the hand scorer's, then measured on the blind sets.
- Validation and gating, stored in `daily_kpi`: `recon_blind_precision`, `recon_blind_recall` (artificial test set), `recon_vendor_pairs_linked` (held-out vendor pairs), `recon_links`, `recon_deaths_linked_share`, `recon_journeys_ge5_tracklets`. If `recon_vendor_pairs_linked < 0.50` or `recon_blind_precision < 0.85` → every per-person KPI of the day gets `status='unreliable', reason='reconciliation_below_gate'` and the pipeline falls back to the hand scorer weights of `config/treviglio.json`. Acceptance 14/09: links 54,510 (± 5 %), deaths linked 0.31, vendor pairs linked 0.68, blind P/R 0.89/0.72 (± 0.03).
- Per-person KPIs (all ESTIMATED, tooltip "su percorsi ricomposti in post-processing; la stessa persona può contare più volte"): `dept_stay_per_person` {persons_touching, persons_per_entrance, stay_median_s, stay_mean_s, stay_p90_s} per department AREA (from `199` §5), `dept_transitions` (STEP C), `queue_time_per_person` distribution bins {<15 s, 15–30, 30–60, 1–2 min, ≥ 2 min}. Acceptance 14/09: Frutta persons_per_entrance 6.2, stay median 8 s; Latticini 3.7 / 11 s; Carne 3.2 / 11 s (± 10 %).
